Travel Impact Calculation

We estimate your travel emissions using your journey distance and the transport method selected. Lower-impact options, such as walking, are used as a baseline, while car and bus journeys are calculated using standard emissions factors. In some cases, factors such as fuel type, fuel efficiency, and passenger occupancy may also be include. This gives you a useful estimate of the carbon impact of your journey, rather than an exact value.

Formula

For most journeys, the calculation uses a simple method:

Estimate CO2e = Distance Travelled × Emissions Factor of Transport Type

How Energy Tracking Works

Log energy-saving actions anytime. To calculate savings, first upload a meter photo of your starting reading. Then submit a new meter image or monthly bill whenever available, and we'll compare the readings to estimate your energy and carbon savings.

Good Energy Actions to Log

  • Used library/study space instead of heating my room
  • Turned heating down for X hours
  • Switched off unused lights/devices

Formula

Estimated CO2e = Energy consumed x emissions factor of the chosen energy type

Food Impact Calculation

We estimate your food-related impact using life cycle assement data for food products. This includes environmental factors such as emissions, land use and water use from production through to processing. Your receipt helps us identify lower-impact choices and generate an estimated saving. These results are intended as an informed estimate, not an exact value.

Formula

Estimated CO2e = Food Consumed x Emissions factor of chosen food type

Waste Impact Calculation

We estimate your waste-related impact based on your recycling habits, the type of materials you recycle, and the size of the bin bag you use. The bin bag size helps us estimate the approximate weight of your recycling. We then apply general conversion factors for different types of recyclable waste to calculate an estimated CO2e saving. These results are intended as informed estimate rather than an exact value.

Formula

Saved CO2e = Reference Basket - Chosen Basket

Chosen Basket = CO2e produced based on your chosen product

Produced CO2e = Product Weight x CO2e Estimation Factor

Reference Basket

A standard higher-carbon shopping basket used as a benchmark for comparison
